# Change Request CR-001

## Request
Hospital asks to add three additional medication workflows immediately before go-live.

## Impact assessment
Scope: High | Schedule: High | Clinical validation: High | Resources: Medium

## Options
1. Add all before go-live — threatens validation and schedule.
2. Add clinically essential item after expedited impact assessment.
3. Defer all enhancements to Phase 2.

## Decision
Defer non-critical enhancements to Phase 2. Assess any clinically necessary workflow separately under expedited clinical validation.

## PM lesson
Do not silently absorb scope. Assess impact on scope, schedule, resources, risk, testing and clinical approval before changing the baseline.
