# Hypercare Plan

**Duration:** 10 business days after go-live.

### Severity model
**P1:** patient-safety/clinical-critical — immediate escalation; affected workflow/content held as appropriate.  
**P2:** major workflow/access issue — same-business-day response.  
**P3:** minor usability/reporting issue — normal prioritization.

### Daily review
Active users, access failures, workflow questions, defects, clinical-content questions, training gaps, enhancements and adoption.

### Exit criteria
No unresolved P1/P2 issues; stable access; adoption >=80%; support ownership transferred; lessons learned captured; benefits review scheduled.
