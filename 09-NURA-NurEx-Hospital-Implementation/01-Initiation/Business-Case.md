# Business Case

## Opportunity
Give ED clinicians structured access to medication information and institution-specific protocols at the point of care.

## Current pain points
1. Multiple information sources
2. Manual lookup/calculation
3. Workflow variation
4. Difficult centralized content governance
5. Limited operational visibility

## Proposed response
Pilot NurEx in the ED with prioritized use cases, validated formulary content, configuration, clinical UAT, training and hypercare.

## Expected benefits
More consistent workflow; faster access to relevant information; lower cognitive burden; centralized institutional content governance; structured user feedback and usage visibility.

## Key risks
Training/adoption, workflow mismatch, clinical-content delays, technical access and scope expansion.

## Recommendation
Use an ED pilot rather than enterprise deployment so readiness and adoption can be measured before expansion.
