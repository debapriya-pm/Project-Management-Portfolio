# Two-Hour Interview Preparation Plan – Program Manager, SPO

This is a rehearsal allocation for a two-hour operations / hiring-manager discussion, not a prediction of the interviewer's agenda.

## Preparation priorities

| Priority | Focus | Rehearsal allocation |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Salesforce primary project narrative and deep dive | 35 minutes |
| 2 | 150-seat / 90-day scenario plan, schedule, WBS and RAID | 25 minutes |
| 3 | ePA secondary project and healthcare workflow | 15 minutes |
| 4 | Stakeholder, risk, budget, resource, change and recovery scenarios | 25 minutes |
| 5 | BPO metrics, governance, MS Project, Excel, PMP/PRINCE2 and methodologies | 10 minutes |
| 6 | Resume probes, STAR examples and closing pitch | 10 minutes |

## Rehearsal sequence

### A. Opening and experience summary
Prepare a 60–90 second summary covering your 9+ years, project/PMO experience, healthcare operations and research exposure, PMP/CSM credentials, cross-functional coordination and why the SPO role is relevant.

### B. Salesforce deep dive
Be able to explain:
- Why the business needed a unified CRM.
- Salesforce configuration versus licence provisioning.
- Avaya CTI, softphone, screen-pop and call logging.
- Data migration, cleansing, mapping and reconciliation.
- SIT versus UAT and defect severity.
- WFM, training, pilot, phased cutover and rollback.
- Governance, RAID, RACI, schedule dependencies and BAU acceptance.
- Which activities you personally own as PM and which the engineers perform.

### C. Scenario planning
Practise walking through the 150-seat / 90-day plan without reading:
1. Understand requirements and feasibility.
2. Charter, scope, acceptance and governance.
3. WBS, schedule, critical path, resource and budget.
4. Risks, dependencies and communication.
5. Recruitment, training, KT and infrastructure readiness.
6. SIT/UAT, pilot, go/no-go and rollback.
7. Hypercare, BAU and lessons learned.

### D. Operational and program questions
Practise short, structured answers for:
- A two-week CTI delay.
- Client asks for earlier go-live but Operations is not ready.
- Recruitment/training misses the plan.
- Budget exceeds baseline.
- Operations refuses BAU handover.
- A key stakeholder misses commitments.
- A failing project needs recovery.
- Competing projects need the same scarce resource.

### E. Metrics and tools
Know the definitions and relationships between SLA, service level, AHT, FCR, CSAT, quality, occupancy, shrinkage, attrition, productivity and abandon rate. Be ready to explain MS Project dependencies/critical path, Excel trackers, Visio process maps, RAG status, RAID and change control.

## Answer structure

For experience questions, use **Situation → Task → Action → Result → Learning**. For scenario questions, use **Clarify → Assess → Options → Decision → Execute → Monitor → Communicate**.

## Final checklist

- I can explain my exact role and decision authority.
- I can distinguish actual work experience from portfolio simulation.
- I can explain every number and its source.
- I can discuss scope, schedule, budget, team size and dependencies without guessing.
- I can describe one successful project, one setback and one issue I personally resolved.
- I can explain how I protect service delivery during transition.
- I can finish answers with business impact and lessons learned.
