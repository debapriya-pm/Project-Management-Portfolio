# Training, Knowledge Transfer and Workforce Readiness Plan

## 1. Objectives

Prepare 150 agents, supervisors and support staff to use Salesforce Service Cloud and the integrated CTI workflow confidently while maintaining live service levels.

## 2. Workstreams

- Training needs analysis by role.
- SOP and knowledge-base development.
- Train-the-Trainer (TTT) and super-user certification.
- Agent training in staggered cohorts.
- Shadowing and reverse shadowing.
- Competency checks and access validation.
- WFM roster and training backfill.
- Floor-walker and hypercare support.

## 3. Training sequence

| Stage | Activities | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Preparation | Identify roles, learning needs, training environment and cohort roster | Approved training plan |
| Content development | Create job aids, SOPs, call/case scenarios and escalation guides | QA-approved materials |
| Train-the-Trainer | Train supervisors and super-users | Attendance and competency |
| Agent classroom / simulation | Practice customer search, screen-pop, case creation, routing and escalation | Assessment record |
| Shadowing | Observe experienced agent handling live contacts | Shadowing checklist |
| Reverse shadowing | Trainee handles contacts under supervisor observation | Signed competency checklist |
| Readiness | Confirm access, schedule, competency and support contact | Agent readiness register |

## 4. WFM and capacity protection

I coordinate with Workforce Management to stagger training cohorts, forecast shrinkage, schedule backfill and monitor queue service levels. I do not remove too many agents from production at once. Training attendance, occupancy, shrinkage and SLA performance are reviewed together.

## 5. Core competency areas

- Salesforce login and role-based access.
- Customer and contact search.
- CTI softphone controls and screen-pop.
- Case creation, categorization and priority.
- Assignment queues and escalation.
- Knowledge search and approved customer communication.
- Call disposition and interaction logging.
- Exception handling and manual fallback.
- Security, privacy and acceptable-use requirements.

## 6. Readiness criteria

- Required training completed.
- Competency assessment passed.
- User account and licence validated.
- CTI softphone and required permissions tested.
- Agent knows escalation path and fallback process.
- Supervisor confirms readiness.

## 7. Change adoption

Use team briefings, supervisor huddles, floor-walkers, FAQ logs, feedback sessions and visible issue resolution. Capture recurring usability problems and prioritize fixes based on customer and operational impact.
