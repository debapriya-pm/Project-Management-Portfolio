# Hypercare and BAU Handover

## 1. Hypercare objectives

Provide heightened support after each deployment wave, stabilize the new workflow, resolve defects quickly and transfer ownership to steady-state Operations and IT support.

## 2. Hypercare operating model

- Daily operational and technical triage.
- Named incident manager and workstream owners.
- Priority-based ticket queue and escalation matrix.
- Floor-walker support for agents and supervisors.
- Daily review of SLA, AHT, FCR, CSAT, abandon rate, case backlog and uptime.
- Defect trend analysis and root-cause review.
- Daily client update during the initial stabilization window.

## 3. Monitoring dashboard

| KPI | Purpose | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| SLA adherence | Verify contractual service performance | Operations / WFM |
| AHT | Detect handling effort or workflow friction | Operations |
| FCR | Assess whether customer needs are resolved on first contact | QA / Operations |
| CSAT | Assess customer experience | QA |
| Abandon rate | Detect queue capacity or telephony problems | WFM |
| Case backlog / aging | Detect case routing and workload issues | Operations |
| CTI error rate | Detect integration instability | IT / CTI |
| Platform uptime | Verify service availability | IT |

## 4. Escalation

- Critical outage/security/data integrity issue: immediate incident escalation to IT and Operations leadership.
- Major service degradation: incident owner, workaround, restoration estimate and frequent stakeholder updates.
- Repeated lower-severity defects: problem record, root-cause analysis and planned corrective action.

## 5. BAU handover package

- Final solution and integration overview.
- Configuration inventory and environment details.
- Approved SOPs and knowledge articles.
- Support model, contact list and escalation matrix.
- Known errors, workarounds and open defect register.
- User/role access and licence administration process.
- Monitoring dashboards and KPI definitions.
- Vendor support and service dependencies.
- Training attendance and competency records.
- Data migration reconciliation and retention evidence.
- Cutover and rollback records.
- Outstanding actions with owners and due dates.

## 6. BAU exit criteria

- SLA adherence at or above 95% for 14 consecutive days (scenario target; contract definition controls).
- Uptime at 99.9% during the agreed measurement window.
- No unresolved Severity 1 defects.
- Severity 2 items accepted with owner, workaround and target date.
- Support team trained and able to operate escalation process.
- Operations and IT accept ownership in writing.
- Client confirms service stabilization.

## 7. Closure

I complete the closure report with scope delivered, milestone performance, cost status, KPI results, accepted exceptions, lessons learned and formal sign-offs. Benefits are reviewed against the baseline after the agreed measurement period.
