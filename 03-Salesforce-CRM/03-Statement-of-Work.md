# Statement of Work (SOW)

**Engagement:** Salesforce Service Cloud Contact Centre Transformation  
**Scenario:** 150-seat BPO / contact-centre transition  
**Planning duration:** 90 calendar days  
**Document status:** Illustrative SOW template – commercial values and approvals are not supplied

## 1. Purpose

Define the scope, deliverables, roles, assumptions, dependencies, acceptance criteria and change-control approach for implementing Salesforce Service Cloud, integrating Avaya CTI and transitioning a 150-agent contact-centre operation.

## 2. Services and work packages

1. Project mobilization, discovery and requirements.
2. Current-state and To-Be process mapping.
3. Salesforce architecture, environment and licence readiness.
4. CRM configuration: objects, fields, page layouts, roles, permissions, cases, queues, assignment, escalation and reports.
5. Avaya CTI integration: softphone, caller identification, screen-pop and call logging.
6. Legacy data inventory, cleansing, mapping, test migration, reconciliation and production migration.
7. SIT, UAT, defect management and regression.
8. SOP development, knowledge transfer, Train-the-Trainer and agent training.
9. WFM capacity planning and training backfill.
10. Cutover rehearsal, pilot, phased rollout, hypercare and BAU handover.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities

**Client**
- Owns business objectives, process decisions and acceptance.
- Provides SMEs, data owners, security contacts and UAT participants.
- Confirms Salesforce licence entitlements and authorizes user access.
- Approves scope, budget, change requests and go/no-go decisions.

**Technology delivery**
- Designs and configures Salesforce.
- Delivers CTI connector and infrastructure changes.
- Performs data migration and technical testing.
- Resolves technical defects and provides support documentation.

**Operations / PMO**
- Maintains integrated plan, governance, RAID, decisions and status reporting.
- Coordinates WFM, recruitment, training, pilot and rollout.
- Manages readiness, cutover communications, hypercare and BAU handover.

**QA / Training / WFM**
- Validates quality requirements and test evidence.
- Develops training and competency checks.
- Protects live queue coverage through staffing and training plans.

## 5. Assumptions and dependencies

- Salesforce orgs, sandbox environments and CTI test environments are available.
- Client provides data extracts and approves data handling rules.
- Network, firewall, identity and security prerequisites are completed on time.
- Vendor support is available during integration and cutover windows.
- Client SMEs make decisions within agreed turnaround times.
- Contractual service levels and measurement definitions are supplied by the client.

## 6. Out-of-scope items

WhatsApp/SMS channel expansion, replacement of Avaya, unapproved custom integrations and unapproved changes to historical data scope are excluded from the core release. They require a documented change request.

## 7. Acceptance process

The Project Manager submits each deliverable with evidence against its acceptance criteria. The named client owner reviews and accepts or rejects it with specific gaps, owner and due date. Conditional acceptance must identify the residual risk and approved workaround.

## 8. Change control

1. Log the request and business rationale.
2. Assess impact on scope, architecture, schedule, cost, resources, quality, security and operations.
3. Obtain estimates from affected workstream owners.
4. Submit to the Change Control Board / authorized client approver.
5. Record approval or rejection.
6. Update baselines, requirements, schedule and communications only after approval.
