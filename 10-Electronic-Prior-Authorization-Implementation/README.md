# Electronic Prior Authorization (ePA) Workflow Implementation

> Secondary healthcare technology case study for interview discussion. Validate the actual project context, your personal responsibilities and all outcome figures before presenting this as a delivered engagement.

## Business context

The scenario addresses a prior-authorization process that relies on manual payer portals, fax and repeated administrative data entry. The intended transformation embeds an electronic prior authorization workflow into the clinical operating environment and coordinates clinical operations, IT integration, billing, compliance and administrative teams.

## Project objective

Design an end-to-end ePA workflow that reduces turnaround time, improves data quality, standardizes exception handling and gives operations a clear view of pending approvals.

## Workstreams

- Requirements and current-state / To-Be workflow mapping.
- EHR and payer clearinghouse integration coordination.
- Clinical data mapping and exception handling.
- SOP standardization and administrative team training.
- Security, privacy and audit coordination.
- SIT/UAT, pilot, hypercare and BAU handover.
- Operational KPI reporting.

## Metrics to validate

The interview scenario previously discussed uses 72 hours as the starting PA turnaround time, under 18 hours as a target, 20% fewer documentation errors and a 35% increase in authorization volume without additional headcount. These are scenario figures unless supported by actual project reports.

## Interview positioning

Use this as the secondary example to demonstrate healthcare workflow knowledge, clinical/administrative stakeholder coordination, integration dependency management, compliance awareness and operational change management. Be specific about what you personally managed and what technical teams delivered.
