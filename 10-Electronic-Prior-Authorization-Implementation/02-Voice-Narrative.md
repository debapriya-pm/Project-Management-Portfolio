# ePA Interview Narrative – Secondary Project

"I use the electronic prior authorization example to demonstrate my understanding of healthcare operations and technology-enabled workflow improvement. The business problem is that prior authorization requests can move through EHR records, payer portals, fax and manual follow-up, creating delays and repeated data entry.

I start by mapping the current process and identifying where requests wait, where staff re-enter information and where exceptions occur. I work with clinical operations, administrative coordinators, billing, IT integration, compliance and medical leadership to define the target workflow, requirements, roles and acceptance criteria.

I break the work into integration, EHR data mapping, clinical protocol and SOP standardization, testing, training, compliance and operational reporting. I maintain the integrated schedule, RAID log, RACI and governance cadence. I coordinate technical teams and business owners rather than claiming to write integration code or make clinical decisions.

I pay particular attention to exception handling. A request may be approved, denied, pending or require additional clinical information. I make sure each outcome has a clear queue, owner, escalation path and status definition. I coordinate SIT and UAT using representative payer and clinical scenarios, then plan training, pilot, hypercare and BAU handover.

I measure the workflow using turnaround time, first-pass completion, documentation error rate, pending-request aging and authorization volume. I only report improvements against a validated baseline and consistent measurement method."
