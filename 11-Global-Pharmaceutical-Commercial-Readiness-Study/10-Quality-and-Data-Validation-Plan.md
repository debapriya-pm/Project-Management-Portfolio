# Quality Assurance and Data Validation Plan

## Qualitative data
- Verify interview completion and respondent eligibility.
- Audit recorded interview transcripts against physician credentials.
- Review transcript completeness and flag inconsistencies.
- Apply a consistent thematic coding framework across markets.

## Quantitative data
- Remove incomplete responses according to the approved protocol.
- Identify and review unusually fast completions (speeders).
- Detect straight-line response patterns.
- Apply documented exclusion rules and retain an auditable cleaning log.

## Safety reconciliation
Reconcile interview disclosures with the adverse-event log and confirm escalation timestamps and client safety receipt records.

## Reporting
The scenario reports 100% transcript verification, three valid adverse-event mentions, an average escalation turnaround of 3.8 hours and zero safety audit observations.
