# Week 7 Recruitment Recovery Plan

## Situation
At the Week 7 checkpoint, hospital-based specialist recruitment in Germany and the United Kingdom was approximately 40% of the time-phased target. Daytime clinical commitments were the primary scheduling barrier.

## Delivery risk
A sustained shortfall threatened the required sample composition, the integrity of cross-market comparisons and the planned executive decision meeting.

## Corrective actions
1. Conduct root-cause review with the German and UK fieldwork managers.
2. Extend interview availability to evening and weekend windows (19:00–21:30).
3. Assign bilingual senior moderators to cover the expanded schedule.
4. Engage specialist regional medical panels in Munich and London.
5. Pre-screen physician credentials before scheduling.
6. Use approved budget contingency to adjust honoraria for hard-to-reach specialist participants.

## Outcome reported in the scenario
Recruitment velocity increased by 180%; the deficit was recovered within 10 days and fieldwork remained on schedule.

## Management controls
Daily quota monitoring during recovery; country-level escalation; approval of contingency spend; and confirmation of eligibility before interview completion.
