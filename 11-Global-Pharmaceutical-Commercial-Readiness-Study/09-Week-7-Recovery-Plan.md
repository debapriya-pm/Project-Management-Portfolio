# Week 7 Recruitment Recovery Plan

## Situation
At the Week 7 checkpoint, hospital-based specialist recruitment in Germany and the UK is tracking at approximately 40% of the time-phased quota. Daytime invitations are competing with clinical rounds and hospital workload. If not corrected, the shortfall threatens market coverage, fieldwork completion and the final analysis window.

## First 48 hours
1. Convene German and UK fieldwork leads and review funnel data by specialty, institution type, invitation channel and time of day.
2. Confirm root cause through recruiter feedback and declined-invitation reasons.
3. Reforecast remaining interviews and available moderator capacity.
4. Present options, cost implications and quality safeguards to the program manager / sponsor.
5. Approve a time-boxed recovery plan with daily measures and named owners.

## Corrective actions
- Offer evening and weekend appointments (scenario window: 19:00–21:30 local time).
- Assign bilingual senior moderators to maintain interview quality and local-language fluency.
- Activate two specialist regional medical panels (scenario locations: Munich and London).
- Pre-verify credentials and eligibility before confirming interview slots.
- Use only pre-approved honorarium ranges; document any contingency drawdown and approval.
- Maintain a reserve list and cancellation backfill process.

## Control tower
| Measure | Review cadence | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified leads | Daily | Below funnel forecast |
| Screen-to-book conversion | Daily | Decline versus baseline |
| Completed interviews vs time-phased quota | Daily | Below 80% of plan |
| Cancellation / no-show rate | Daily | Above agreed threshold |
| Moderator capacity and utilization | Daily | Capacity bottleneck |
| Contingency spend | Twice weekly | Forecast exceeds approved reserve |

## Scenario result
The case narrative records a 180% increase in recruitment velocity and full recovery within 10 days, with all planned fieldwork completed within the 14-week timeline. These are illustrative scenario outcomes, not independently verified operational data.

## Lessons
- Design recruitment windows around clinician workflows.
- Keep reserve panels and moderators available for specialist populations.
- Track funnel conversion, not only final completed interviews.
- Escalate early using time-phased quota thresholds.
- Protect respondent eligibility and data quality while accelerating volume.
