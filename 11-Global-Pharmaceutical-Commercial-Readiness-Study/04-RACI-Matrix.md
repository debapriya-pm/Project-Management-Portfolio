# RACI Matrix

**R = Responsible | A = Accountable | C = Consulted | I = Informed**

| Activity | Program Manager | Research Lead | Country Leads | Compliance / Privacy | PV Contact | Data / QA | Client Sponsor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope and charter | R | C | I | C | I | I | A |
| Research protocol and instruments | A | R | C | C | C | C | I |
| Local translation and adaptation | A | R | R | C | I | I | I |
| Privacy and ethics review | C | C | C | A/R | I | I | I |
| AE escalation workflow | A | C | R | C | R | I | I |
| Country recruitment and quotas | A | C | R | I | I | C | I |
| Interview moderation | I | A | R | I | C | C | I |
| Survey programming and launch | A | R | C | C | I | R | I |
| Data cleaning and quality gate | A | C | I | C | I | R | I |
| Findings and recommendations | A | R | C | I | I | C | C |
| Executive acceptance | R | C | I | I | I | I | A |

## Decision rights
- Program Manager owns integrated plan, escalation, cross-workstream dependencies and change control.
- Research Lead owns research-methodology recommendations and analytical integrity.
- Compliance / Privacy Lead can pause activities pending required review or remediation.
- Pharmacovigilance contact owns client-side safety assessment after receipt of a report.
- Client Sponsor approves material scope, budget or timeline changes and final strategic deliverables.
