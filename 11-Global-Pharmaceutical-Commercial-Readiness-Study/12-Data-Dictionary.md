# Data Dictionary and Workbook Guide

## Source workbook worksheets

| Worksheet | Purpose | Key fields |
|---|---|---|
| Executive_Dashboard | Program dossier, methodology and research objectives | Study scope, budget, timeline, sample design, objectives |
| 14Week_Fieldwork_Schedule | Work breakdown and 14-week delivery schedule | WBS code, task, lead, duration, start / end week, milestone, status |
| Sample_&_Recruitment_Tracker | Country-level recruitment and screening | Market, KOL target / complete, payer target / complete, survey target / complete |
| Adverse_Event_Log | Safety incident and escalation audit | Incident ID, market, description, timestamps, TAT, audit verdict |
| Key_Findings_&_Insights | Commercial findings and recommendations | Theme, finding, sentiment, risk, recommendation |

## Dashboard calculations
- Respondents completed = sum of completed KOL, payer / pharmacy and quantitative survey participants.
- Respondent target = sum of target KOL, payer / pharmacy and quantitative survey participants.
- Quota attainment = completed respondents divided by target respondents.
- AE turnaround = source-reported average of recorded incident turnaround times.

## Interpretation
All figures are drawn from the supplied workbook and voice-script scenario. Values are illustrative and should not be treated as independently verified commercial or clinical evidence.
