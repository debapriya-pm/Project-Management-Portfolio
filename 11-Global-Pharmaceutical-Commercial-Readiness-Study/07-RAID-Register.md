# RAID Register — Key Program Risks and Controls

| ID | Type | Risk / dependency | Potential impact | Response / control | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | Risk | Hospital specialist recruitment below time-phased quota in Germany and UK | Sample shortfall and delay to synthesis | Evening/weekend slots, bilingual senior moderators, pre-screened regional panels | Program Manager / Fieldwork Leads |
| R02 | Risk | Inconsistent country interpretation of research instruments | Reduced cross-market comparability | Translation, cultural adaptation and local review | Research Lead |
| R03 | Risk | Adverse event mentioned during interview is not escalated promptly | Safety and compliance exposure | Mandatory 24-hour study escalation workflow and incident log | Program Manager / Quality |
| R04 | Risk | Low-quality or incomplete survey responses | Biased or unreliable quantitative findings | Screen incomplete responses, speeders and straight-line responses | Data Operations |
| R05 | Risk | Transcript or respondent authenticity issue | Invalid qualitative evidence | Verify transcripts and prescriber credentials | QA Specialist |
| D01 | Dependency | Ethics / privacy review and localized tools completed before fieldwork | Fieldwork start gate | Stage-gate sign-off before launch | Compliance / Research |
| D02 | Dependency | Client safety department available to receive AE reports | Timely escalation | Confirm named contacts and receipt process before fieldwork | Program Manager |
| A01 | Assumption | Required specialist panels can supply eligible participants | Recruitment plan feasibility | Validate panel capacity and screen credentials | Fieldwork Leads |
| I01 | Issue | Week 7 recruitment at approximately 40% of time-phased target in Germany and UK | Delivery risk | Activate recruitment recovery plan | Program Manager |
