# Executive Findings & Strategic Recommendations

> The figures below are illustrative case-study data. They are not clinical evidence, validated market estimates or claims about a real product.

| Theme | Illustrative finding | Commercial risk | Recommended response | Proposed measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Efficacy perception | 78% of specialist respondents rate the profile favorably | Positive intent may be moderated by safety concerns | Equip field medical teams with balanced, approved evidence addressing efficacy and safety questions | Message comprehension and follow-up inquiry themes |
| Payer access | All 15 interviewed payers anticipate step-therapy requirements | Delayed access and administrative burden | Develop payer evidence package and clinic-facing prior-authorization support | PA completeness, time to decision, denial / appeal themes |
| Administration | 84% prefer a monthly home auto-injector over hospital IV infusion | Dexterity, confidence and technique concerns may affect initiation | Include trainer device and clear nurse / clinic education pathway | Training completion and device-confidence feedback |
| Affordability | Survey scenario indicates prescribing intent falls as monthly out-of-pocket cost rises above $50 | Prescription abandonment / non-initiation | Evaluate compliant affordability support options with market access and legal review | Abandonment reasons and affordability-related inquiries |

## Strategic narrative
The central executive message is that physician enthusiasm alone does not guarantee launch uptake. Access restrictions, patient administration confidence and affordability can interrupt the path from prescription intent to treatment initiation.

## Recommended action sequence
1. **Market access:** Map payer evidence requirements and build a prior-authorization documentation toolkit.
2. **Clinic enablement:** Pilot a support concierge model to help clinics navigate administrative steps.
3. **Patient onboarding:** Test an ergonomic trainer device and teach-back education for self-administration.
4. **Affordability:** Assess lawful, market-specific support approaches with compliance and market access teams.
5. **Measurement:** Define launch-readiness indicators and monitor access friction, training completion and abandonment signals.

## Decision traceability
Each recommendation should have an evidence reference, confidence rating, accountable business owner, implementation dependency and success measure in the final client deck. Avoid presenting directional interview evidence as causal proof.
