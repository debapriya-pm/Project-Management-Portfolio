# Potential Adverse Event (AE) Escalation SOP — Study Workflow

> This is a portfolio-level workflow example, not a replacement for the sponsor's pharmacovigilance SOP, medical judgement, or local legal requirements. The 24-hour target below is the scenario's internal escalation SLA; confirm the applicable sponsor procedure and local requirements before real-world use.

## Objective
Ensure any potential adverse event, product complaint or other safety information disclosed during research is captured and routed promptly to the sponsor's designated safety function.

## Workflow
1. **Recognize:** Moderator or recruiter hears a possible AE / product complaint mention.
2. **Do not investigate clinically:** Do not assess causality, severity or expectedness; do not offer medical advice.
3. **Capture minimum permitted information:** Use sponsor-approved form and avoid unnecessary patient identifiers. Record respondent reference, product reference, event description as volunteered, awareness date/time, country and contact route where permitted.
4. **Timestamp and secure:** Enter the event in the restricted safety log; limit access to authorized personnel.
5. **Escalate:** Notify the designated sponsor PV contact through the approved secure route within the study's 24-hour internal target, or faster if required by the sponsor SOP / local rules.
6. **Confirm receipt:** Record acknowledgement, recipient and timestamp. Escalate to backup contact if no acknowledgement within the defined internal interval.
7. **Retain evidence:** Preserve the audit trail according to the sponsor-approved retention schedule.
8. **Close operational loop:** Confirm handoff completeness without taking over the sponsor's medical assessment.

## Controls
- Moderator training and acknowledgement before fieldwork.
- Primary and backup PV contacts tested before launch.
- Restricted-access log with timestamp fields.
- Daily review of open handoffs during fieldwork.
- Documented escalation and acknowledgement evidence.
- Periodic spot-check by quality / compliance lead.
