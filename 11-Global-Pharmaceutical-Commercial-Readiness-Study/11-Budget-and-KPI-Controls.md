# Budget & KPI Controls

## Financial baseline
- Scenario fixed-price contract baseline: **USD 485,000**.
- Actual expenditure, invoicing schedule, margin and category-level budget are not provided in the source scenario and are therefore not represented as actuals.

## Recommended budget control structure
| Control | Operating approach |
|---|---|
| Baseline | Approved scope, fee, cost assumptions and contingency recorded at kickoff |
| Forecast | Monthly and milestone-based estimate to complete; weekly during recovery |
| Variance | Track actual vs baseline, committed cost and estimate at completion |
| Change control | Written approval before scope, sample, incentive or schedule changes affect cost |
| Contingency | Draw only against documented trigger, rationale and delegated approval |
| Closeout | Reconcile supplier invoices, deliverable acceptance and lessons learned |

## Core delivery KPIs
| KPI | Definition | Scenario / target |
|---|---|---|
| Sample attainment | Completed eligible respondents ÷ target respondents | 332 ÷ 325 = 102.2% (rounding in source workbook: 102.1%) |
| KOL quota attainment | Completed KOL interviews ÷ target | 62 ÷ 60 = 103.3% |
| Payer quota attainment | Completed payer interviews ÷ target | 15 ÷ 15 = 100% |
| Survey quota attainment | Completed surveys ÷ target | 255 ÷ 250 = 102% |
| Schedule adherence | Completion by baseline milestone | 14-week scenario completion |
| AE escalation TAT | Time from awareness to sponsor safety notification | Scenario average ~3.8 hours vs internal 24-hour target |
| Data quality | Records passing defined QA rules ÷ records reviewed | Threshold set in approved analysis plan |
| Budget variance | (Actual cost − baseline) ÷ baseline | Actuals not supplied; monitor against approved tolerance |

## Data reconciliation note
The workbook rounds the total sample attainment to 102.1%; the arithmetic 332 / 325 is approximately 102.15%, which rounds to 102.2% at one decimal place using conventional rounding. Use one consistent rounding convention across the final deck and tracker. The voice script's “102%” is a reasonable whole-number summary.

## Governance thresholds (proposed)
- Forecast cost variance greater than 5%: sponsor review.
- Milestone forecast slip greater than five working days: recovery plan and escalation.
- Recruitment below 80% of time-phased plan: corrective action.
- Any potential AE: immediate SOP-based escalation, independent of routine KPI cadence.
