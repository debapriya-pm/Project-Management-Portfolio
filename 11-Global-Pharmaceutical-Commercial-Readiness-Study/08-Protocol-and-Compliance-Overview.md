# Research Protocol and Compliance Overview

## Study design
Mixed-methods research combining semi-structured 60-minute interviews with specialist physicians and payer / hospital-pharmacy stakeholders, plus a structured online physician survey.

## Physician screening criteria
- Board-certified specialist in the relevant clinical area.
- At least three years of biologic or advanced-therapy prescribing experience.
- Minimum active severe-patient caseload threshold as specified in the source screener.
- Medical registry and hospital-affiliation verification.
- Conflict-of-interest screening and required declarations.

## Research instrument controls
Discussion guides and survey instruments are reviewed, tested and localized for Germany, France and Italy. Localized instruments undergo cultural adaptation before field deployment.

## Compliance controls
The project dossier identifies EphMRA, BHBIA and GDPR as applicable research and privacy frameworks. Operational controls include participant screening, consent and privacy safeguards, restricted handling of respondent information, and documented safety escalation.

## Pharmacovigilance workflow
Any potential adverse event disclosed during research is documented and escalated to the client's drug-safety contact under the study's 24-hour operating procedure. The source workbook records three incidents and an average escalation turnaround of 3.8 hours.

**Important:** This document summarizes the supplied scenario. It is not legal advice or a substitute for country-specific regulatory review, sponsor SOPs or approved pharmacovigilance procedures.
