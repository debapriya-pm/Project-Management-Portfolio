# Project Charter — Global Biologic Commercial Readiness Study

## Purpose
Assess physician adoption potential, payer access requirements and patient-administration considerations for a newly approved biologic therapy ahead of commercial launch.

## Objectives
- Assess specialist perceptions of efficacy and safety relative to standard of care.
- Understand payer and hospital-formulary requirements, including step therapy and prior authorization.
- Evaluate physician preference for administration route and dosing frequency.
- Identify patient journey friction and support requirements.
- Deliver evidence-based recommendations to global commercial leadership.

## Scope
**Markets:** United States, United Kingdom, Germany, France and Italy.  
**Duration:** 14 weeks.  
**Illustrative budget:** USD 485,000.  
**Research design:** 60 target KOL interviews, 15 payer / hospital-pharmacy interviews and 250 physician survey responses.

## Key deliverables
Approved protocol and screening tools; localized research instruments; completed qualitative interviews and quantitative survey; validated dataset; pharmacovigilance log; executive findings and recommendations.

## Governance
The Lead Program Manager coordinates client alignment, schedule, cross-country fieldwork, recruitment performance, quality controls, safety escalation and executive delivery. Functional leads own research, fieldwork, compliance, translation, data operations and quality activities.

## Success measures
Completion of the 14-week schedule; achievement of planned sample quotas; validated research data; documented safety escalation within the study's 24-hour operating protocol; and delivery of the executive findings workshop.

## Assumptions and controls
Budget, respondent counts, findings and outcomes are scenario data from the supplied project dossier. The study requires appropriate consent, privacy controls, research-code compliance and pharmacovigilance handling.
