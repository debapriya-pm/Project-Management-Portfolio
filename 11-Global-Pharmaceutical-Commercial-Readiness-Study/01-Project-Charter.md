# Project Charter

## 1. Project purpose
Support commercial launch planning for a newly approved injectable biologic by generating structured evidence on physician adoption, payer access, administration preferences and pricing sensitivity across five markets.

## 2. Objectives
- Assess physician perception of efficacy, safety and target product profile.
- Understand payer and hospital pharmacy access criteria, including step therapy and documentation.
- Identify administration and patient-support barriers.
- Quantify prescribing intent and price sensitivity.
- Deliver prioritized recommendations to the global commercial leadership team by the end of Week 14.

## 3. Scope

**In scope**
- US, UK, Germany, France and Italy.
- 60 KOL specialist interviews, 15 payer / hospital pharmacy interviews and 250 online physician surveys (targets).
- Screener, discussion guide, questionnaire and translations.
- Recruitment, fieldwork coordination, quality assurance, data cleaning, synthesis and executive workshop.
- Study-specific privacy, ethics and adverse-event escalation procedures.

**Out of scope**
- Clinical trials or treatment-effectiveness evaluation.
- Patient recruitment or direct patient interviews.
- Regulatory submission or product-label decisions.
- Negotiation of payer contracts or reimbursement decisions.
- Medical advice or individual patient case management.

## 5. Governance
- **Executive sponsor:** Global Commercial Brand Director (scenario role).
- **Program manager:** Integrated delivery owner.
- **Research lead:** Methodology, instruments and analysis.
- **Country fieldwork leads:** Local recruitment and scheduling.
- **Compliance / privacy lead:** Ethics, privacy and local code review.
- **Pharmacovigilance contact:** Client drug-safety escalation recipient.
- **Data / QA lead:** Data validation, transcript and survey quality.

**Cadence**
- Weekly client status and decision review.
- Twice-weekly country quota review during active recruitment.
- Daily exception review during critical fieldwork recovery.
- Stage-gate sign-off at protocol, fieldwork completion, data lock and final delivery.

## 6. Success criteria
- Complete the agreed sample or obtain documented client approval for any deviation.
- Complete fieldwork and executive delivery within 14 weeks.
- Maintain traceable screening, consent, privacy and quality records.
- Escalate potential adverse events within the study's approved SOP timeframe.
- Deliver recommendations linked to evidence, commercial risk and accountable action.

## 7. Key assumptions and constraints
- Sponsor provides approved product information and safety contact details.
- Local recruitment panels can access eligible specialists.
- Country-specific privacy, research-code and consent requirements are reviewed before launch.
- All budget and performance figures in this portfolio case are illustrative.
- No actual contract, client identity or confidential information is represented.
