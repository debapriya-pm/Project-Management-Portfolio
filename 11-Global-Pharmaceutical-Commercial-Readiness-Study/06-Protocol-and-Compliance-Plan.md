# Research Protocol & Compliance Plan

## 1. Research approach
A mixed-method design combines qualitative exploration with quantitative validation. Qualitative interviews explore decision drivers and barriers; the physician survey tests the prevalence of themes within the recruited sample. Findings should be interpreted within the limits of the sample design and recruitment approach.

## 2. Illustrative respondent eligibility
- Board-certified specialist in a relevant disease area.
- At least three years of biologic / advanced-therapy prescribing experience.
- Active management of a defined minimum monthly patient caseload (scenario threshold: 30).
- Verified professional credentials and relevant practice affiliation.
- Conflict-of-interest screening and documented eligibility decision.

Final criteria must be agreed with the sponsor and reviewed for local appropriateness before recruitment.

## 3. Instruments
- Semi-structured 60-minute KOL and payer interview guides.
- Structured online physician survey (scenario duration: approximately 25 minutes).
- Country-specific screeners, consent language and privacy notices.
- Translation and cultural adaptation for German, French and Italian; local medical review and pilot before use.

## 4. Ethics, privacy and research codes
- GDPR is a legal data-protection framework applicable where relevant; it is not interchangeable with voluntary industry research codes.
- EphMRA and BHBIA are industry guidance / codes relevant to healthcare market research, subject to applicability and current versions.
- Confirm local legal basis, transparency, data minimization, retention, access control, international transfer safeguards and vendor responsibilities with qualified privacy / legal stakeholders.
- Obtain informed participation consent and explain recording, use, confidentiality and withdrawal rights.
- Do not collect patient-identifiable information. Instruct respondents not to disclose identifiable patient details.
- Separate professional respondent data from research responses where feasible; restrict access by role.

## 5. Pharmacovigilance readiness
- Train moderators and recruiters to recognize possible adverse-event and product-complaint mentions.
- Explain the approved intake fields, internal notification route, backup contact and time recording.
- Follow the sponsor's approved pharmacovigilance SOP and applicable local requirements; the scenario uses a 24-hour internal escalation target.
- Do not assess causality, provide medical advice or delay reporting while seeking clarification.
- Document acknowledgement and retain a restricted audit trail.

## 6. Quality gates
- Gate 1: sponsor approval of objectives, protocol, instruments and sample plan.
- Gate 2: compliance/privacy review, local language approval and safety workflow readiness.
- Gate 3: sample and fieldwork completion review.
- Gate 4: data cleaning, transcript QA and analysis dataset sign-off.
- Gate 5: final report, recommendation traceability and sponsor acceptance.
