# Quality Assurance & Data Validation Plan

## Qualitative stream
- Verify respondent eligibility against the approved screener and professional credential evidence.
- Confirm interview completion, duration and required sections.
- Review recordings / transcripts under the approved consent and retention arrangements.
- Use a standard transcript QA checklist; resolve omissions and transcription anomalies.
- Develop a codebook with clear definitions, inclusion / exclusion rules and examples.
- Double-code a subset and reconcile material differences.
- Maintain an audit trail from raw transcript to coded theme and reported insight.

## Quantitative stream
- Define exclusion rules before reviewing outcomes.
- Remove or flag incomplete records against pre-agreed completion thresholds.
- Identify speeders using a pilot-informed threshold rather than an arbitrary universal cut-off.
- Review straight-lining alongside response variance and logical consistency; do not exclude solely on one indicator without review.
- Check duplicates, eligibility, routing, missingness and outliers.
- Maintain a disposition log with reason codes and counts.
- Freeze and version the cleaned dataset before analysis.

## Reporting controls
- Distinguish respondent counts from unique individuals where applicable.
- Report denominators alongside percentages.
- Avoid generalizing a purposive or panel-based sample to the entire physician population without a defensible sampling basis.
- Treat 15 payer interviews as qualitative directional evidence, not a statistically representative payer census.
- Link each recommendation to evidence, commercial implication, confidence and proposed owner.
- Clearly label all scenario figures as illustrative in the portfolio.

## Acceptance checklist
- [ ] Sample disposition reconciled to target and completed counts.
- [ ] Eligibility and consent evidence available.
- [ ] Transcript QA completed and exceptions resolved.
- [ ] Survey exclusions documented and reproducible.
- [ ] Codebook and thematic matrix approved.
- [ ] Safety handoffs reconciled to restricted safety log.
- [ ] Findings traceable to source data and denominators.
- [ ] Final deliverables version-controlled and sponsor-approved.
