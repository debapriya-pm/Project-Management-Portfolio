# Governance and Responsibility Matrix

| Workstream / decision | Lead Program Manager | Research Lead | Fieldwork Leads | Compliance / QA | Data Operations | Client Sponsor |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Scope, objectives and milestones | A/R | C | I | C | I | A/C |
| Protocol and research instruments | A | R | C | C | C | C |
| Country recruitment and quotas | A | C | R | I | C | I |
| Ethics, privacy and safety workflow | A | C | I | R | I | C |
| Translation and localization | A | R | C | C | I | I |
| Data cleaning and validation | A | C | I | C | R | I |
| Findings and recommendations | A/R | R | C | C | R | C |
| Executive acceptance and delivery | R | C | I | I | C | A |

**Legend:** R = Responsible; A = Accountable; C = Consulted; I = Informed. This is a portfolio governance view based on the roles described in the project schedule; final client-specific decision rights would be confirmed during kickoff.
