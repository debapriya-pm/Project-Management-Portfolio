## Escalation thresholds
- Any potential safety event: immediate internal routing under the approved SOP; do not wait for routine status meeting.
- Recruitment below 80% of the time-phased quota: recovery review and corrective action owner.
- Forecast milestone slip greater than five working days: sponsor escalation with recovery options.
- Forecast budget variance greater than 5%: change-control review before commitment.
- Material privacy or data-quality concern: pause affected activity pending compliance / QA decision.
