# 5. Key Dependencies

The following dependencies will be actively monitored:

- Charter approval must occur before detailed planning.
- Research objectives must be approved before instrument development.
- Research instruments must be approved before fieldwork begins.
- Participant recruitment must progress before data collection can be completed.
- Data quality validation must occur before analysis is finalized.
- Analysis must be completed before final reporting.
- Stakeholder validation must occur before final recommendations are approved.

---

# 8. Schedule Management

Project progress will be monitored against the approved baseline.

Schedule monitoring will include:

- Planned versus actual milestone dates
- Upcoming activities
- Delayed activities
- Critical dependencies
- Resource constraints
- Schedule risks
- Corrective actions

Material schedule deviations will be escalated through the appropriate governance process.

---

# 9. Change Management

Changes to approved scope, timelines, methodology, or major deliverables will be assessed for:

- Business impact
- Timeline impact
- Resource impact
- Budget impact
- Research quality impact
- Stakeholder impact

Significant changes will require appropriate stakeholder approval before implementation.

---

# 10. Project Success Measures

The project will be monitored against:

- Milestone adherence
- Deliverable completion
- Recruitment progress
- Research quality
- Stakeholder satisfaction
- Risk resolution
- Schedule variance
- Timeliness of reporting
