# Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program

## Project Overview

A simulated end-to-end healthcare market research program designed to demonstrate practical capabilities across project management, healthcare research, business analysis, data analytics, and executive reporting.

The project is structured around a global pharmaceutical market research study focused on understanding physician perceptions, adoption likelihood, effectiveness, administrative burden, safety perceptions, and related market insights.

---

## Project Objective

The objective is to demonstrate how a healthcare research program can be structured and managed from initial business need through research design, project planning, data analysis, insights generation, and executive reporting.

---

## Project Lifecycle

The project follows a structured delivery approach:

**Business Problem → Project Initiation → Research & Requirements → Planning & Governance → Data Collection → SQL / PostgreSQL Analysis → Power BI & DAX → Business Insights → Executive Reporting → Lessons Learned**
