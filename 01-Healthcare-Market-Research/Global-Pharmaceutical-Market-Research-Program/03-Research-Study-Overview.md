# Research Study Overview

## Study Title

Physician Treatment Decision-Making and Unmet Needs Study

## Parent Program

Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program

## Client

NovaCura Pharmaceuticals (Fictional)

## Consulting Partner

InsightBridge Healthcare Consulting

## Study Type

Mixed-Method Healthcare Market Research

---

# 1. Study Background

NovaCura Pharmaceuticals is seeking to better understand physician treatment decision-making within a selected therapeutic area.

The study is designed to explore how physicians evaluate treatment options, the challenges they encounter in clinical practice, and the factors that influence treatment selection and adoption.

The research combines qualitative and quantitative methodologies to provide both contextual understanding and measurable evidence.

---

# 2. Business Question

The primary business question is:

> What factors influence physician treatment decisions, and where do meaningful opportunities exist to improve treatment adoption and address unmet needs?

---

# 3. Research Objectives

The study aims to:

1. Understand physician treatment decision-making processes.
2. Identify key treatment selection drivers.
3. Explore unmet needs within the current treatment pathway.
4. Understand barriers to adoption of newer treatment options.
5. Assess perceptions of competing therapies.
6. Measure the relative importance of identified decision drivers.
7. Identify differences across physician segments.
8. Translate research findings into actionable commercial insights.

---

# 4. Target Audience

The research will focus on relevant healthcare stakeholders, including:

- Physicians
- Specialists
- Key opinion leaders
- Other relevant healthcare professionals

The exact participant profile would be determined during the study design stage based on the therapeutic area and business objectives.

---

# 5. Research Methodology

The study uses a sequential mixed-method approach.

## Phase 1 — Qualitative Research

Qualitative research will be conducted first to explore attitudes, behaviors, motivations, and unmet needs in depth.

### Potential Method

Semi-structured in-depth interviews with physicians.

### Qualitative Objectives

The interviews will explore:

- Treatment decision journeys
- Patient management challenges
- Treatment selection criteria
- Perceptions of available therapies
- Unmet needs
- Barriers to treatment adoption
- Competitive perceptions
- Desired improvements in treatment options

### Qualitative Output

The qualitative phase will generate:

- Key themes
- Behavioral insights
- Treatment decision drivers
- Unmet need areas
- Barriers and opportunities
- Hypotheses for quantitative validation

---

# 6. Phase 2 — Quantitative Research

The quantitative phase will validate and measure themes identified during qualitative research.

## Potential Method

Structured online survey among a defined physician sample.

## Quantitative Measures

The survey may measure:

- Awareness
- Consideration
- Treatment preferences
- Importance of decision criteria
- Product perceptions
- Competitive perceptions
- Adoption barriers
- Unmet needs
- Likelihood of adoption
- Physician segmentation variables

---

# 7. Mixed-Method Integration

The two research phases will be integrated rather than analyzed independently.

### Example

The qualitative phase may identify:

**Administrative burden** as an important barrier to treatment adoption.

The quantitative phase can then measure:

- How frequently physicians identify administrative burden as a barrier
- Its relative importance compared with other barriers
- Differences across physician segments
- Differences across geographic markets

This allows the research team to move from:

**"Why is this happening?"**

to:

**"How widespread and important is it?"**

# 10. Key Research Questions

The study will seek to answer questions such as:

### Treatment Decisions

- What factors most influence treatment selection?
- How does treatment choice vary across physician segments?
- What role do patient characteristics play?

### Unmet Needs

- What challenges remain insufficiently addressed?
- Which aspects of the treatment pathway create the greatest friction?

### Competitive Landscape

- How are available treatment options perceived?
- What strengths and weaknesses are associated with competing therapies?

### Adoption

- What prevents physicians from adopting newer treatment options?
- What conditions could increase willingness to adopt?

Research findings will be organized into four major areas:

### 1. Decision Drivers

What drives treatment selection?

### 2. Barriers

What prevents adoption or creates friction?

### 3. Unmet Needs

Where are current treatment approaches falling short?

### 4. Opportunities

Where could the client potentially create greater value?

---

# 12. Example Insight

### Finding

Physicians indicate that treatment effectiveness remains the primary consideration, but administrative complexity can discourage adoption of otherwise attractive treatment options.

### Interpretation

Treatment decisions are influenced by both clinical and operational considerations.

### Potential Business Implication

Commercial teams may need to consider not only clinical value but also the broader treatment experience and operational burden when developing positioning and support strategies.
