# Business Case

## Project

Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program

---

# Executive Summary

NovaCura Pharmaceuticals (fictional) is preparing its 2027 commercial strategy across multiple therapeutic areas, including Oncology, Diabetes, and Immunology.

To support evidence-based decision-making, the organization requires a structured market research program consisting of multiple qualitative and quantitative studies.

This program will generate actionable insights into physician behavior, patient experiences, competitive dynamics, treatment pathways, and market opportunities.

The objective is to improve strategic decision-making while ensuring research activities are delivered efficiently through standardized project governance and program management practices.

---

# Business Background

Healthcare markets are evolving rapidly due to changing treatment guidelines, increasing competition, new product launches, and evolving patient expectations.

Business leaders require reliable market intelligence to support commercial planning, product positioning, lifecycle management, and future investment decisions.

Managing multiple research studies independently increases the risk of inconsistent reporting, duplicated effort, scheduling conflicts, and delayed insights.

---

# Problem Statement

The organization currently manages multiple market research studies independently, creating challenges in:

- Resource coordination
- Timeline management
- Stakeholder communication
- Progress visibility
- Risk tracking
- Executive reporting

Without a centralized program management approach, project delays and inconsistent governance may impact business decisions.

---

# Business Objectives

The program aims to:

- Coordinate multiple healthcare market research studies through a structured governance model.
- Improve collaboration between research teams and business stakeholders.
- Standardize project reporting.
- Increase visibility into project status and risks.
- Deliver high-quality insights within agreed timelines.

---

# Proposed Solution

Establish a centralized Project Management Office (PMO) approach to oversee the complete research portfolio.

The program will introduce standardized planning, stakeholder communication, risk management, status reporting, and performance monitoring across all studies.

---

# Expected Benefits

- Improved project visibility
- Better stakeholder communication
- More predictable delivery timelines
- Reduced project risks
- Efficient resource utilization
- Higher-quality decision support for business leaders

---

# Risks of Not Proceeding

If the program is not implemented, the organization may experience:

- Delayed strategic decisions
- Poor visibility across research initiatives
- Resource conflicts
- Increased operational inefficiencies
- Reduced confidence in research outcomes

---

# Success Measures

The program will be considered successful if:

- 95% of research milestones are achieved on schedule.
- Stakeholder satisfaction exceeds 90%.
- Executive status reports are delivered on time.
- Project risks are proactively managed.
- Cross-functional collaboration improves throughout the program.

---

# Recommendation

Proceed with the Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program using a structured project governance framework to improve coordination, reporting, stakeholder engagement, and strategic decision-making across the research portfolio.
