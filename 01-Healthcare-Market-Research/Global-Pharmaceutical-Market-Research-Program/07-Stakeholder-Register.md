# Stakeholder Register

## Project

**Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program**

## Purpose

The stakeholder register identifies key stakeholders involved in the research program, their roles, level of influence, information needs, and engagement approach.

The purpose is to ensure that stakeholders receive the right information at the right time and remain appropriately engaged throughout the program lifecycle.

---

## Stakeholder Overview

| Stakeholder | Role / Interest | Influence | Level of Interest | Engagement Approach |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Executive Sponsor | Provides strategic direction and funding | High | High | Manage closely |
| Commercial Leadership | Uses research findings to support commercial strategy | High | High | Manage closely |
| Medical Affairs | Provides clinical and therapeutic-area input | High | High | Manage closely |
| Market Research Lead | Owns research methodology and insight quality | High | High | Manage closely |
| Project Manager / Coordinator | Coordinates timelines, dependencies, deliverables and communication | High | High | Manage closely |
| Research Partners | Conduct qualitative and quantitative research activities | Medium | High | Collaborate closely |

| Regional Market Teams | Provide market-specific requirements and context | Medium | Medium | Keep informed and engaged |
| Finance / Procurement | Supports vendor management and budget controls | Medium | Low | Keep satisfied |
| Legal / Compliance | Reviews research compliance and governance requirements | High | Medium | Consult regularly |
| External Participants | Physicians / healthcare stakeholders participating in research | Low | High | Engage appropriately |

## Stakeholder Analysis

### 1. Executive Sponsor

**Primary interest:** Strategic alignment, business value, budget, and successful delivery.

**Key information required:**
- Program progress
- Major risks and issues
- Budget status
- Key milestones
- Emerging research findings
- Decisions requiring executive input

**Engagement approach:**
- Executive status reporting
- Steering committee updates
- Escalation of critical risks and decisions

---

### 2. Commercial Leadership

**Primary interest:** Actionable market insights supporting commercial strategy and planning.

**Key information required:**
- Market trends
- Customer / physician insights
- Competitive intelligence
- Treatment pathway insights
- Key implications for commercial strategy

**Engagement approach:**
- Insight reviews
- Interim findings
- Executive presentations
- Final recommendation workshops

---

### 3. Market Research Lead

**Primary interest:** Research quality, methodology, timelines, and insight generation.

**Key information required:**
- Study progress
- Recruitment status
- Research quality
- Methodology changes
- Data collection issues
- Analysis status

**Engagement approach:**
- Regular working sessions
- Research progress reviews
- Issue resolution meetings
- Deliverable reviews

---

### 4. Project Manager / Coordinator

**Primary interest:** Predictable delivery across multiple concurrent research workstreams.

**Key responsibilities:**
- Timeline management
- Dependency tracking
- Stakeholder coordination
- Risk and issue management
- Meeting coordination
- Status reporting
- Deliverable tracking
- Cross-functional communication

**Engagement approach:**
- Centralized project tracking
- Weekly status reporting
- RAID management
- Regular project meetings

---

### 5. Research Partners

**Primary interest:** Clear scope, research requirements, timelines, and deliverable expectations.

**Key information required:**
- Study objectives
- Research methodology
- Sample requirements
- Milestones
- Deliverable expectations
- Feedback and approval timelines

**Engagement approach:**
- Kick-off meetings
- Weekly working sessions
- Deliverable reviews
- Action tracking

## Stakeholder Communication Cadence

| Communication | Audience | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Project Team Meeting | Core project team | Weekly | Progress, actions and dependencies |
| Research Workstream Review | Research teams | Weekly | Study-level progress |
| Steering Committee Update | Executive stakeholders | Bi-weekly / Monthly | Strategic decisions and escalation |
| Status Report | Key stakeholders | Weekly | Overall program health |
| Risk & Issue Review | Project leadership | Weekly | Review and mitigation |
| Insight Review | Commercial / Medical stakeholders | At key milestones | Validate emerging insights |
| Final Readout | Executive and business stakeholders | End of program | Present findings and recommendations |

---

## Engagement Principles

The stakeholder engagement strategy is based on the following principles:

1. Align stakeholders early on objectives, scope, roles, and expectations.
2. Maintain transparent communication across concurrent research workstreams.
3. Escalate material risks and decisions promptly.
4. Tailor communication to stakeholder needs and level of influence.
5. Provide business stakeholders with actionable insights rather than research data alone.
6. Maintain clear ownership of actions, decisions, and deliverables.
7. Ensure research activities remain aligned with the overall business objective.

---

## Project Management Perspective

Effective stakeholder management is particularly important for a multi-study pharmaceutical market research program because the program involves multiple business functions, research partners, therapeutic areas, geographic markets, and concurrent timelines.

The project coordinator / project manager acts as the central coordination point to maintain visibility across workstreams, manage dependencies, facilitate communication, and ensure that research outputs remain aligned with business objectives.
