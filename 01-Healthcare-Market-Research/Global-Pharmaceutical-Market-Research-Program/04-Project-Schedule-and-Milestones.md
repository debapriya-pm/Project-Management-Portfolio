# Project Schedule & Milestones

## 1. Schedule Purpose

This project schedule translates the Work Breakdown Structure (WBS) into a time-phased implementation plan.

The schedule demonstrates how project activities can be sequenced, monitored, and controlled across the lifecycle of a pharmaceutical market-research program.

The timeline is an illustrative portfolio-project schedule and is not intended to represent the actual timeline of a client engagement.

---

## 2. Project Timeline

**Planned Duration:** 12 Weeks

**Project Approach:** Hybrid project-management approach combining structured planning, iterative analysis, stakeholder review, and incremental dashboard development.

## 6. Major Dependencies

The following dependencies are considered critical:

### Planning Dependencies

Project planning activities depend on completion of project initiation and objective definition.

### Data Dependencies

Quantitative analysis depends on successful completion of data assessment and preparation.

### Dashboard Dependencies

Dashboard development depends on validated analytical results and defined KPI calculations.

### Reporting Dependencies

Executive reporting depends on completion and interpretation of the analytical findings.

### Closure Dependencies

Project closure depends on final deliverable validation, documentation, and lessons learned.

---

## 7. Critical Sequence

A simplified critical sequence for the project is:

**Project Charter**

→ **Scope & Requirements**

→ **WBS**

→ **Data Assessment**

→ **Data Preparation**

→ **Quantitative Analysis**

→ **KPI Validation**

→ **Power BI Development**

→ **Dashboard QA**

→ **Insights**

→ **Executive Reporting**

→ **Project Closure**

Any significant delay in data preparation or KPI validation could affect downstream dashboard development and reporting activities.

## 8. Project Monitoring Approach

Project progress can be monitored using:

* Planned vs. completed activities
* Milestone completion
* Deliverable status
* Dependency tracking
* Risk status
* Scope changes
* Stakeholder feedback
* Quality review results

A project tracker can be used to classify activities as:

* Not Started
* In Progress
* At Risk
* Completed

---

## 9. Schedule Assumptions

The schedule assumes:

* Required research data is available at project initiation.
* Stakeholder requirements can be collected within the planned timeframe.
* Data preparation does not require extensive remediation.
* Dashboard development proceeds after KPI definitions are validated.
* Stakeholder reviews occur within planned windows.
* No major scope changes occur during execution.

---

## 10. Schedule Risks

Potential schedule risks include:

| Risk                      | Potential Schedule Impact | Mitigation                                   |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Poor data quality         | Delays analysis           | Perform early data-quality assessment        |
| Unclear requirements      | Rework                    | Validate requirements early                  |
| KPI definition changes    | Dashboard rework          | Establish KPI definitions before development |
| Stakeholder review delays | Reporting delay           | Establish review deadlines                   |
| Scope expansion           | Timeline extension        | Apply change-control process                 |
| Dashboard defects         | Delivery delay            | Conduct structured QA before finalization    |

---

## 11. Schedule Governance

The Project Manager is responsible for:

* Maintaining the project schedule
* Tracking milestone completion
* Monitoring dependencies
* Identifying schedule variance
* Escalating significant delays
* Coordinating stakeholder reviews
* Updating project status
* Managing schedule-related risks

---

## 12. Schedule Success Criteria

The schedule will be considered effectively managed when:

1. Major project activities are clearly sequenced.
2. Dependencies are identified.
3. Key milestones are defined.
4. Deliverables are linked to project activities.
5. Schedule risks are actively monitored.
6. Significant deviations are identified early.
7. Final project deliverables are completed within the planned 12-week portfolio timeline.
