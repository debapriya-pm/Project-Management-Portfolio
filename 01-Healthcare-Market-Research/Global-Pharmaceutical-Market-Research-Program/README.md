# Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program

## Overview

This portfolio case study demonstrates the planning and coordination of a global pharmaceutical market research program supporting strategic business decisions for a multinational pharmaceutical client.

The program consists of multiple concurrent qualitative and quantitative research studies designed to generate insights into physician behavior, patient experiences, market dynamics, competitive positioning, and treatment pathways.

As the Project Coordinator, my role is to support the successful delivery of the research portfolio by coordinating timelines, facilitating communication between stakeholders, monitoring project risks, tracking deliverables, and ensuring studies are executed according to agreed plans.

---

## Business Challenge

The client requires reliable market intelligence to support commercial strategy, product planning, and evidence-based decision-making across multiple therapeutic areas.

Managing multiple research studies simultaneously requires structured governance, effective stakeholder communication, resource planning, and proactive risk management.

---

## Objectives

- Deliver multiple market research studies on schedule.
- Maintain high-quality research deliverables.
- Coordinate cross-functional teams.
