# Qualitative Discussion Guide

## Study

Physician Treatment Decision-Making and Unmet Needs Study

## Parent Program

Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program

## Method

Semi-Structured In-Depth Physician Interviews

## Client

NovaCura Pharmaceuticals (Fictional)

---

# 1. Research Purpose

The purpose of the qualitative interviews is to understand physician treatment decision-making, current treatment experiences, unmet needs, barriers to adoption, and perceptions of available treatment options.

The interviews are exploratory and are intended to generate deep contextual insights that can subsequently inform quantitative research.

---

# 2. Research Objectives

The interviews aim to explore:

1. Current treatment decision-making processes.
2. Factors influencing treatment selection.
3. Patient and physician challenges within the treatment pathway.
4. Unmet needs.
5. Perceptions of available treatment options.
6. Barriers to adoption.
7. Competitive perceptions.
8. Opportunities for improving the treatment experience.

---

| Section | Topic | Indicative Time |

# 4. Introduction

## Moderator Script

Thank you for taking the time to participate in this discussion.

We are conducting research to better understand treatment decision-making and experiences within the relevant therapeutic area.

There are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in your professional experience, perspectives, and observations.

Your responses will be analyzed in aggregate for research purposes.

# 5. Physician Background

### Q1

Could you briefly describe your current clinical role and area of specialization?

### Probe

- Years of experience
- Patient population
- Practice setting
- Relevant therapeutic experience

### Q2

Approximately what proportion of your practice involves patients within this therapeutic area?

### Probe

- Patient volume
- Patient types
- Disease severity

# 7. Treatment Decision Journey

### Probe

- Initial assessment
- Diagnosis
- Treatment selection
- Treatment initiation
- Monitoring
- Treatment adjustment
- Switching

### Q6

What factors are most influential at each stage?

### Probe

- Clinical factors
- Patient preferences
- Previous treatment experience
- Guidelines

- Access considerations

### Probe

---

# 14. Moderator Guidance

The moderator should:

- Remain neutral.
- Avoid leading questions.
- Encourage detailed responses.
- Use open-ended questions before probes.
- Explore unexpected themes.
- Avoid suggesting preferred answers.
- Clarify ambiguous responses.
- Maintain the interview structure while allowing natural discussion.
- Capture emerging themes consistently.
