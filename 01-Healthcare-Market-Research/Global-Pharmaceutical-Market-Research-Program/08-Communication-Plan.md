# Communication Plan

## Program

Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program

## Client

NovaCura Pharmaceuticals (Fictional)

## Consulting Partner

InsightBridge Healthcare Consulting

## Program Role

Project Coordinator

---

## 1. Purpose

The communication plan establishes a structured approach for sharing project information with stakeholders throughout the Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program.

The objective is to ensure that stakeholders receive the right information, at the right frequency, through the appropriate communication channel.

---

## 2. Communication Principles

The program will follow these principles:

- Communication should be timely and relevant.
- Stakeholders should receive information appropriate to their role.
- Critical risks and issues should be escalated promptly.
- Decisions and actions should be documented.
- Executive communication should focus on outcomes, risks, decisions, and business impact.
- Project teams should have sufficient detail to execute their responsibilities.

---

| Stakeholder / Audience | Information Required | Frequency | Method | Owner |

| Executive Sponsor | Overall status, major risks, decisions, milestones | Monthly / As required | Executive review | Project Coordinator |

| Regional Teams | Market-specific progress and requirements | Bi-weekly | Regional review | Project Coordinator |

## 4. Project Status Reporting

A weekly project status report will provide visibility into:

- Overall project status
- Completed activities
- Activities planned for the next period
- Key milestones
- Schedule performance
- Risks
- Issues
- Dependencies
- Decisions required
- Actions requiring attention

---

## 5. Executive Reporting

Executive updates will focus on information required for decision-making.

The executive summary will include:

### Overall Status

A high-level assessment of project health.

### Key Achievements

Major milestones or deliverables completed during the reporting period.

### Upcoming Milestones

Important activities and decisions expected during the next reporting period.

### Key Risks

Material risks that could affect scope, timeline, quality, or business outcomes.

### Key Issues

Issues requiring management attention or escalation.

### Decisions Required

Specific decisions needed from senior stakeholders.

---

## 6. Meeting Cadence

### Weekly Project Coordination Meeting

Participants:

- Project Coordinator
- Research Lead
- Workstream Leads
- Data / Analytics representatives

Purpose:

- Review progress
- Review upcoming activities
- Track actions
- Review risks and issues
- Identify dependencies
- Escalate concerns

---

### Bi-Weekly Stakeholder Review

Participants:

- Commercial stakeholders
- Research Lead
- Project Coordinator
- Relevant workstream leads

Purpose:

- Review research progress
- Validate requirements
- Discuss emerging findings
- Review risks and decisions

---

### Monthly Executive Review

Participants:

- Executive Sponsor
- Commercial Leadership
- Research Leadership
- Project Coordinator

Purpose:

- Review overall program health
- Review milestones
- Review major risks and issues
- Obtain executive decisions
- Confirm strategic alignment

---

## 7. Escalation Communication

Issues will be escalated when they exceed agreed thresholds for:

- Timeline impact
- Scope impact
- Budget impact
- Research quality
- Compliance
- Stakeholder impact

Escalation communication should clearly state:

1. The issue or risk
2. Business impact
3. Current status
4. Options available
5. Recommended action
6. Decision required
7. Required decision date

---

## 8. Decision Management

Important project decisions will be documented in a decision log.

Each decision should include:

- Decision ID
- Date
- Decision required
- Options considered
- Decision made
- Decision owner
- Business rationale
- Impact on project

---

## 9. Action Management

Actions identified during meetings will be tracked using an action log.

Each action should include:

- Action ID
- Description
- Owner
- Due date
- Status
- Related workstream
- Escalation requirement

---

## 10. Communication Risks

Potential communication risks include:

- Stakeholders not receiving timely updates
- Unclear ownership of actions
- Delayed decision-making
- Inconsistent information across markets
- Important risks not being escalated
- Excessive communication without clear business value

These risks will be managed through defined communication cadence, ownership, documentation, and escalation procedures.

---

## 11. Success Measures

Communication effectiveness will be evaluated through:

- Timely delivery of project updates
- Stakeholder attendance and engagement
- Timely closure of actions
- Timely stakeholder decisions
- Reduced communication-related delays
- Clear documentation of decisions
- Stakeholder satisfaction
