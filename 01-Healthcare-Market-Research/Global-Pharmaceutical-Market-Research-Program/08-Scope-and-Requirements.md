# Scope & Requirements

## 1. Purpose

The Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program is designed to evaluate physician perspectives, adoption potential, perceived effectiveness, administrative burden, and safety considerations associated with pharmaceutical products and healthcare solutions.

The project combines structured market research, stakeholder analysis, quantitative data analysis, and business intelligence to provide actionable insights for pharmaceutical and healthcare decision-makers.

---

## 2. Business Objective

The primary objective is to transform physician survey data into meaningful business insights that can support pharmaceutical market strategy, product positioning, stakeholder engagement, and future research planning.

### Key objectives

* Analyze physician perspectives across relevant pharmaceutical and healthcare dimensions.
* Identify factors influencing adoption likelihood.
* Evaluate perceived product effectiveness and safety.
* Assess administrative burden experienced or anticipated by physicians.
* Identify meaningful patterns and trends within the research dataset.
* Translate research findings into an executive-friendly Power BI dashboard.
* Provide a structured project-management framework for conducting and delivering the research program.

---

## 3. Project Scope

### In Scope

The project includes:

* Project initiation and planning
* Stakeholder identification and analysis
* Market-research data assessment
* Physician survey data analysis
* Data preparation and validation
* Quantitative analysis
* KPI development
* Power BI dashboard development
* Business insight generation
* Risk identification and management
* Project documentation
* Executive-level reporting
* Recommendations based on analyzed findings

### Out of Scope

The following activities are outside the scope of this portfolio project:

* Clinical trials
* Direct patient treatment or medical advice
* Regulatory submission activities
* Drug manufacturing or supply-chain management
* Primary physician interviews conducted specifically for this portfolio project
* Financial forecasting for pharmaceutical products
* Actual commercial launch execution
* Real-world prescribing recommendations

## 6. Functional Requirements

### FR-01 — Physician Data Analysis

The solution shall analyze physician survey data to identify meaningful patterns and trends.

### FR-02 — Adoption Analysis

The solution shall calculate and display physician adoption likelihood.

### FR-03 — Effectiveness Analysis

The solution shall evaluate perceived effectiveness ratings.

### FR-04 — Administrative Burden Analysis

The solution shall measure and communicate perceived administrative burden.

### FR-05 — Safety Analysis

The solution shall analyze physician safety ratings.

### FR-06 — KPI Reporting

The dashboard shall provide high-level KPIs that allow stakeholders to quickly understand the overall results.

### FR-07 — Interactive Visualization

The dashboard shall allow users to explore research findings through interactive visualizations.

### FR-08 — Executive Reporting

The project shall translate analytical findings into concise business insights suitable for management-level review.

---

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements

### NFR-01 — Usability

The dashboard and project outputs should be easy for business stakeholders to understand.

### NFR-02 — Accuracy

Calculated KPIs should accurately represent the underlying survey data.

### NFR-03 — Consistency

Data definitions, calculations, terminology, and reporting formats should remain consistent across project deliverables.

### NFR-04 — Maintainability

The analytical model should be structured so that the dashboard can be updated when new or revised data becomes available.

### NFR-05 — Documentation

Major assumptions, calculations, project decisions, and deliverables should be documented.

### NFR-06 — Professional Presentation

Final project outputs should follow professional project-management and business-reporting standards.

---

## 8. Key KPIs

The Power BI analysis includes the following core KPIs:

* **Total Physicians**
* **Average Adoption Likelihood**
* **Average Effectiveness**
* **Average Administrative Burden**
* **Average Safety Rating**

These KPIs provide a high-level view of physician perceptions and create a foundation for deeper analysis.

---

## 9. Assumptions

The project is based on the following assumptions:

* The physician survey dataset is sufficiently representative for portfolio analysis.
* Survey responses are assumed to have been collected using a consistent methodology.
* Data fields have sufficient quality for analytical purposes.
* Rating scales are interpreted consistently across relevant variables.
* The dataset does not represent clinical or regulatory evidence.
* Dashboard findings are intended for analytical and illustrative purposes rather than clinical decision-making.
* Business recommendations are based solely on the available research data and project assumptions.

---

## 10. Constraints

Potential project constraints include:

* Limited availability of research data
* Potential data-quality issues
* Limited contextual information surrounding individual survey responses
* Time limitations for analysis and reporting
* Reliance on an existing dataset
* Limited ability to validate findings through additional primary research

---

## 11. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

1. Project scope and objectives are clearly documented.
2. Key stakeholders and responsibilities are identified.
3. Survey data is analyzed using a structured methodology.
4. Core business KPIs are successfully calculated.
5. A professional Power BI dashboard is completed.
6. Findings can be communicated clearly to non-technical stakeholders.
7. Project risks and assumptions are documented.
8. Project deliverables are organized in a professional portfolio structure.
9. The final repository demonstrates practical application of project-management, healthcare research, and data-analysis skills.

---

## 12. Traceability

The project follows a basic requirements-to-deliverable traceability approach:

**Business Objective → Requirement → Analysis → KPI → Dashboard → Insight → Recommendation**

This ensures that analytical outputs remain connected to the original business objectives rather than functioning as standalone visualizations.

---

## 13. Project Boundaries

The project focuses specifically on physician-centered pharmaceutical market research and related business insights.

It does not attempt to establish clinical efficacy, regulatory approval, patient outcomes, or prescribing recommendations.

The purpose of the analysis is to demonstrate how a Project Manager can coordinate research, data analysis, stakeholder requirements, and business reporting within a pharmaceutical market-research environment.
