# Program Charter

## 1. Program Purpose

The Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program is designed to generate actionable market and customer insights to support strategic commercial decision-making across multiple therapeutic areas.

The program brings together multiple qualitative and quantitative market research studies into a coordinated research portfolio.

The research will examine areas including:

- Physician behavior and perceptions
- Patient experiences and treatment journeys
- Market dynamics
- Competitive positioning
- Treatment pathways
- Unmet needs
- Product awareness and adoption drivers

---

## 2. Business Need

NovaCura Pharmaceuticals requires reliable and structured market intelligence to support its future commercial strategy.

Multiple research studies may be conducted concurrently across therapeutic areas, markets, and stakeholder groups.

A coordinated program management approach is required to:

- Align research activities with business objectives
- Coordinate multiple concurrent studies
- Manage timelines and dependencies
- Maintain stakeholder alignment
- Track risks, issues, and decisions
- Support consistent reporting
- Ensure research outputs are delivered on schedule

---

## 3. Program Objectives

The program objectives are to:

1. Develop a structured portfolio of qualitative and quantitative research studies.
2. Generate actionable insights for commercial and strategic decision-making.
3. Coordinate multiple research workstreams and external partners.
4. Maintain visibility into timelines, dependencies, risks, and issues.
5. Establish a consistent stakeholder communication and governance approach.
6. Support high-quality and timely delivery of research outputs.

---

## 4. Scope

### In Scope

The program includes:

- Research planning
- Study coordination
- Qualitative research
- Quantitative research
- Stakeholder coordination
- Research vendor coordination
- Participant/recruitment tracking
- Data and analysis coordination
- Insight synthesis
- Research reporting
- Project status reporting
- Risk and issue management
- Governance and decision tracking

### Out of Scope

The program does not include:

- Pharmaceutical product development
- Clinical trial management
- Regulatory submission activities
- Manufacturing operations
- Product pricing decisions
- Direct medical advice or patient treatment decisions

---

The program will produce:

- Research business case
- Program charter
- Stakeholder register
- Research plan
- Project schedule
- Risk and issue register
- Communication plan
- Qualitative research outputs
- Quantitative research outputs
- Research findings and insights
- Executive presentation
- Program closure summary

---

## 6. Key Stakeholders

Key stakeholders include:

- Pharmaceutical client sponsor
- Commercial strategy team
- Market research lead
- Project coordination team
- Qualitative research team
- Quantitative research team
- Data and analytics team
- Medical and scientific experts
- Fieldwork and recruitment partners
- Compliance and legal stakeholders
- Regional and country teams
- Senior leadership

---

## 7. Governance

The program will use a structured governance approach consisting of:

### Executive Governance

Senior stakeholders provide strategic direction, resolve major escalations, and review key program outcomes.

### Program Management

The Project Coordinator maintains program-level visibility across timelines, dependencies, risks, issues, actions, and decisions.

### Research Workstreams

Qualitative and quantitative research teams manage study-level execution and provide regular progress updates.

### Escalation

Material risks, timeline deviations, scope changes, or unresolved issues are escalated through the appropriate governance channel.

---

## 8. Success Criteria

The program will be considered successful when:

- Research studies are delivered within agreed timelines.
- Key stakeholders remain aligned throughout the program.
- Research quality requirements are maintained.
- Risks and issues are proactively identified and managed.
- Research findings are clearly communicated.
- Outputs provide actionable insights for business decision-making.
- Program dependencies are effectively coordinated.

---

## 9. Assumptions

The program assumes that:

- Business stakeholders will provide timely input and decisions.
- Research participants can be recruited according to study requirements.
- External research partners will meet agreed deliverables.
- Required data and research inputs will be available.
- Study requirements may vary by therapeutic area and market.
- Appropriate governance and approval processes will be followed.

---

## 10. Key Risks

Potential program-level risks include:

- Delays in participant recruitment
- Stakeholder availability constraints
- Changes in research requirements
- Delays from external research partners
- Data quality issues
- Cross-market coordination challenges
- Timeline conflicts between concurrent studies
- Delays in stakeholder approvals

---

## 11. High-Level Timeline

The program follows a phased delivery approach:

1. Program initiation
2. Research planning
3. Study design
4. Participant recruitment and fieldwork
5. Data collection
6. Analysis
7. Insight development
8. Stakeholder review
9. Final reporting
10. Program closure

---

## 12. Program Constraints

The program must operate within:

- Agreed research timelines
- Available stakeholder capacity
- Research budgets
- Participant recruitment availability
- Data quality requirements
- Applicable compliance and governance requirements

---

## 13. Approval

This charter establishes the overall direction, objectives, scope, governance approach, and success criteria for the Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program.
