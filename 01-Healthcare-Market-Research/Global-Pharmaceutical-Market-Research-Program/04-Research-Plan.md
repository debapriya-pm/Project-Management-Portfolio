# Research Plan

## Program

Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program

## Client

NovaCura Pharmaceuticals (Fictional)

## Consulting Partner

InsightBridge Healthcare Consulting

---

## 1. Research Purpose

The purpose of the research program is to generate actionable market insights that support commercial strategy, customer understanding, and evidence-based decision-making across selected pharmaceutical markets and therapeutic areas.

The program combines qualitative and quantitative research methodologies to provide both depth of understanding and measurable market evidence.

---

## 2. Research Objectives

The research program aims to understand:

- Customer needs and unmet needs
- Physician perceptions and preferences
- Patient treatment journeys
- Treatment decision drivers
- Barriers to product adoption
- Competitive positioning
- Awareness and perceptions of pharmaceutical brands
- Market trends and emerging opportunities
- Differences across geographic markets
- Factors influencing treatment decisions

---

# 3. Research Approach

The program uses a mixed-method research approach.

### Qualitative Research

Qualitative research will provide depth and context around stakeholder attitudes, behaviors, motivations, and experiences.

Potential methods include:

- In-depth interviews
- Focus groups
- Expert interviews
- Patient journey discussions
- Concept testing
- Semi-structured interviews

### Quantitative Research

Quantitative research will provide measurable evidence across larger samples.

Potential methods include:

- Online surveys
- Structured questionnaires
- Physician surveys
- Patient surveys
- Market sizing inputs
- Segmentation analysis
- Descriptive statistical analysis

---

# 6. Mixed-Method Integration

Qualitative and quantitative findings will be integrated to develop a comprehensive understanding of the market.

### Example

Qualitative research may identify:

> Physicians perceive administrative burden as a significant barrier to adoption.

Quantitative research can then measure:

- Percentage of physicians reporting the barrier
- Differences across specialties
- Differences across geographic markets
- Relative importance compared with other barriers

This combination provides both **depth and measurable evidence**.

# 12. Research Risks

Key risks include:

| Risk | Potential Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Recruitment delays | Timeline slippage | Monitor recruitment weekly and maintain contingency options |
| Low participant quality | Reduced research reliability | Strengthen screening and quality checks |
| Changing research objectives | Scope changes | Establish formal change control |
| Delayed stakeholder feedback | Approval delays | Define review timelines in advance |
| Data quality issues | Incorrect insights | Perform structured data validation |
| Cross-market differences | Inconsistent findings | Establish standardized research framework with local adaptation |
