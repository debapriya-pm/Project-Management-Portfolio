# RACI Matrix

## 1. Purpose

The RACI Matrix defines roles and responsibilities for major activities within the Global Pharmaceutical Market Research Program.

The matrix is designed to establish accountability, improve cross-functional coordination, reduce ambiguity, and support effective project governance.

---

## 2. RACI Definitions

| Role                | Meaning                                                   |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **R — Responsible** | Person or team responsible for performing the work        |
| **A — Accountable** | Person ultimately accountable for the outcome or decision |
| **C — Consulted**   | Person or group whose input is required                   |
| **I — Informed**    | Person or group who should receive updates                |

### Important RACI Principle

Each major activity should ideally have **one clear Accountable owner**, even when multiple people are Responsible or Consulted.

## 5. Responsibility by Project Phase

### Project Initiation

The Project Manager leads project initiation and coordination, while the Business Sponsor provides strategic direction and accountability.

### Project Planning

The Project Manager coordinates scope, requirements, schedule, WBS, risks, and governance activities with input from research, healthcare, analytics, and business stakeholders.

### Research & Data Preparation

The Market Research Lead and Data / Analytics Lead provide the primary research and analytical expertise.

The Project Manager remains accountable for coordination, dependencies, and delivery.

### Analysis

The Data / Analytics Lead is responsible for quantitative analysis and KPI validation, while the Market Research Lead and Healthcare / Pharma SME provide contextual input.

### Dashboard Development

The BI / Power BI Analyst is responsible for dashboard development and visualization.

The Data / Analytics Lead supports KPI logic and analytical validation.

The Project Manager remains accountable for delivery.

### Insights & Reporting

The Market Research Lead and Healthcare / Pharma SME contribute to interpretation, while the Project Manager coordinates the final outputs.

Business and executive stakeholders provide feedback and decision context.

### Project Closure

The Project Manager coordinates final validation, documentation, lessons learned, and closure.

---

## 6. RACI Governance Principles

The project follows these governance principles:

1. Every major activity should have a clearly identified accountable owner.
2. Responsibilities should be assigned before execution begins.
3. Stakeholders should be consulted when their expertise is required.
4. Stakeholders should not be unnecessarily involved in operational activities.
5. Executive stakeholders should receive concise information relevant to decisions.
6. RACI assignments should be reviewed if project scope or team responsibilities change.

---

## 7. RACI Success Criteria

The RACI Matrix will be considered effective when:

* Major project activities have clear ownership.
* Accountability is not ambiguous.
* Cross-functional responsibilities are understood.
* Stakeholder involvement is appropriate.
* Project decisions have identifiable owners.
* Teams understand who must be consulted or informed.
* Responsibility gaps and overlaps are minimized.
