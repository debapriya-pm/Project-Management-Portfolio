# Implementation Glossary

**Discovery:** Understand current state, users, pain points, constraints and goals.

**Requirements:** What the solution must do and the constraints it must satisfy.

**Baseline:** Approved version of scope, requirements or schedule used for change control.

**Configuration:** Setting up an existing product to match approved client requirements.

**Integration:** Connecting systems so information can move between them.

**UAT:** User Acceptance Testing; business/clinical users verify agreed workflows.

**Defect:** Failure to meet expected behavior or requirement.

**Go-live:** Production launch.

**Cutover:** Controlled sequence moving from readiness to live operation.

**Hypercare:** Enhanced support immediately after go-live.

**RAID:** Risks, Assumptions, Issues and Decisions.

**RTM:** Requirements Traceability Matrix.

**Change Control:** Structured assessment and approval of changes to the baseline.

**Go/No-Go:** Evidence-based readiness decision.

**Super User:** Trained representative supporting peers and escalating issues.

**Benefits Realization:** Checking whether intended outcomes occurred.
