# Go / No-Go Criteria

All critical gates must be green:

1. Clinical content validated and approved
2. Critical UAT scenarios passed
3. Zero unresolved critical defects
4. Access/security validation complete
5. Super users trained
6. >=95% target users trained or approved coverage plan
7. Support/escalation path published
8. Cutover checklist complete
9. Hospital sponsor and vendor implementation lead approve readiness

**No-Go:** unresolved critical clinical-content discrepancy, critical access/security issue, critical UAT failure without approved workaround, no trained super-user coverage, or no support/escalation model.

The PM consolidates evidence and escalates; clinical and technical decision makers own their respective approvals.
